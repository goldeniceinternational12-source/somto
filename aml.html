Executive Summary

Goldenice International Limited ("the Company") is fully committed to preventing money laundering and terrorist financing through its comprehensive Anti-Money Laundering (AML) program. This policy establishes the framework for identifying, assessing, and mitigating financial crime risks across all business operations.

Policy Objectives

  • Comply with all applicable AML/CTF laws and regulations (FCA, FINCEN, FATF recommendations)
  • Establish robust Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) procedures
  • Implement real-time transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting
  • Maintain comprehensive record-keeping and audit trails
  • Ensure ongoing staff training and awareness programs

1
Scope and Applicability

Policy Coverage

This AML Policy applies to all employees, officers, directors, agents, and appointed representatives of Goldenice International Limited. It covers all products and services offered, including:

  • Forex and CFD trading services
  • Payment processing and transfers
  • Corporate and institutional accounts
  • Introducing broker arrangements

Regulatory Framework

The Company operates under multiple regulatory regimes and adheres to:

UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017 FCA Handbook SYSC 6.3 FATF 40 Recommendations EU 5th Money Laundering Directive US BSA / USA PATRIOT Act

  • • Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their associates
  • • Customers from high-risk jurisdictions (FATF grey/black list)
  • • High-net-worth individuals (HNWIs) with complex ownership
  • • Corporate entities with nominee shareholders or bearer shares
  • • Cash-intensive businesses

Additional EDD measures: Senior management approval, enhanced ongoing monitoring, source of funds verification, quarterly relationship reviews.

For corporate and institutional clients:

  • • Certificate of incorporation / registration
  • • Memorandum and Articles of Association
  • • Register of directors and shareholders (verified)
  • • Identification of beneficial owners (>25% ownership)
  • • Board resolution authorizing account opening
  • • ID verification for all authorized signatories

3
Transaction Monitoring & Screening

Real-Time Monitoring

  • Automated transaction monitoring system (TMS) screening all trades
  • Threshold-based alerts for unusual activity patterns
  • Velocity checks (frequency and volume of transactions)
  • Geographic risk indicators and cross-border transfer monitoring

Red Flag Indicators

  • Structuring transactions to avoid reporting thresholds
  • Rapid movement of funds (in and out) without clear purpose
  • Transactions inconsistent with customer's profile or stated business
  • Use of third-party accounts or unexplained third-party payments

4
Suspicious Activity Reporting (SAR)

Tipping Off Offense

It is a criminal offense under UK law to "tip off" a customer that a SAR has been filed or that they are under investigation. All staff must maintain strict confidentiality regarding SAR filings. Internal discussions should be limited to the MLRO and authorized compliance personnel only.

SAR Filing Requirements

When to File:

  • • Knowledge/suspicion of money laundering
  • • Reasonable grounds to suspect terrorist financing
  • • Attempted transactions above thresholds
  • • Customer behavior inconsistent with profile

Timeline:

  • • Immediate: Terrorist financing suspicions
  • • Within 24 hours: Urgent ML suspicions
  • • Within 30 days: Standard ML suspicions
  • • Defense SAR: Prior to disclosure
