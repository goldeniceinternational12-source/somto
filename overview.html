Who We Are

Company Overview

Goldenice International is a premier provider of restaurant equipment, café solutions, and food processing systems headquartered in Nigeria, Rivers State, Port-Harcourt.

Excellence in Hospitality Solutions

Founded in 2025, Goldenice International has rapidly grown to become one of the leading companies in the field of Stock market and Real Estate, as well as packaging and processing systems. We operate under the highest international standards, holding HACCP and GMP certifications.

Our mission is to empower Stock Trading businesses with Trading equipment, innovative packaging solutions, and comprehensive processing systems that drive efficiency and quality.
