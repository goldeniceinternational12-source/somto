Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our complaints process

What qualifies as a complaint? ▼

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, whether oral or written, about the provision of, or failure to provide, a financial service. This includes issues with our trading platform, trade execution, account management, customer service, fees, or any other aspect of our business relationship.

Is there a cost to submit a complaint? ▼

No. Submitting a complaint to Goldenice International is completely free of charge. Furthermore, if you escalate to the Financial Ombudsman Service, their service is also free for consumers. You do not need to hire a lawyer or pay any fees to have your complaint heard.

Can I complain on behalf of someone else? ▼

Yes, but you must provide written authorization from the account holder. This can be a signed letter or power of attorney document. We may also require identity verification for both you and the account holder to protect against unauthorized access.

What information should I include in my complaint? ▼

To help us investigate efficiently, please include: (1) Your full name and account number, (2) Clear description of the issue with dates and times, (3) Any relevant order numbers or ticket IDs, (4) Screenshots or supporting documents if applicable, (5) What resolution you are seeking, and (6) Your preferred contact method.

What if I'm not satisfied with the resolution? ▼

If you disagree with our final response, you have the right to escalate to the Financial Ombudsman Service within six months. The Ombudsman provides an independent assessment and their decision is binding on us (if you accept it). Alternatively, you may seek legal advice or pursue court proceedings, though this may incur costs.

Will my complaint affect my account or trading? ▼

Absolutely not. Submitting a complaint will never negatively impact your account status, trading conditions, or relationship with Goldenice. We view complaints as valuable feedback that helps us improve our services. All complaints are handled confidentially and professionally.
