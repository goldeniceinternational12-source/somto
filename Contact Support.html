Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common investor inquiries

Withdrawals can be requested through your Client Portal under "Funds" > "Withdraw". We process withdrawals within 1-3 business days. Funds are returned to the original source of deposit where possible. For bank transfers, please ensure your bank details are verified. Minimum withdrawal is $50. No fees for withdrawals over $500.
A margin call occurs when your account equity falls below the required margin maintenance level (typically 100% of used margin). You will receive an alert to deposit additional funds or close positions. If your margin level reaches 50% (Stop Out), our system will automatically close your largest losing positions to prevent negative balance.
Account verification requires: (1) Valid government-issued ID (Passport/Driver's License), (2) Proof of Address (Utility bill or bank statement dated within 3 months), (3) Selfie holding your ID. Upload documents through the Client Portal under "Profile" > "Verification". Processing typically takes 4-6 hours during business days.
We offer MetaTrader 5 (MT5) for desktop and mobile, our proprietary WebTrader platform (no download required), and API access for algorithmic traders. All platforms feature real-time execution, advanced charting, and one-click trading. Demo accounts available for practice.
Client funds are held in segregated accounts with Tier-1 banks (Barclays, HSBC) and never used for operational purposes. We are regulated by the FCA (UK) and participate in the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS), providing protection up to £85,000 per eligible client. We also maintain professional indemnity insurance.
