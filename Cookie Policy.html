What Are Cookies?

Understanding the basics of cookie technology

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the website owners.

At Goldenice International, we use cookies to enhance your trading experience, improve our platform's functionality, analyze site traffic, and personalize content. Cookies help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our platform, and ensure the security of your account.

🔍 How Long Do Cookies Last?

Cookies can be either "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them). We use both types to optimize your trading experience.

Your Privacy Rights

Understanding your control over personal data

Under data protection laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable regulations), you have the following rights regarding cookies and your personal data:

👁️

Right to Know

You have the right to know what cookies are being used, what data they collect, and with whom it is shared.

🗑️

Right to Delete

You can clear cookies from your browser at any time. Note that this may affect platform functionality.

🚫

Right to Object

You have the right to object to the use of marketing and analytics cookies at any time.

📱

Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through settings. Visit your browser's help section for instructions.

⚠️ Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable certain cookies, some features of our platform may not function properly. For example, disabling necessary cookies will prevent you from logging in or executing trades. Disabling preference cookies will reset your customizations on each visit.
