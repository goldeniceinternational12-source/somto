Risk Categories

Understanding different types of geopolitical threats

⚔️

Military Conflict

Armed conflicts, territorial disputes, and military tensions that disrupt trade routes, energy supplies, and investor confidence. Often cause immediate flight to safe-haven currencies.

Recent Examples
• Russia-Ukraine War (2022-present)
• Gaza Conflict escalation (2023)
• Armenia-Azerbaijan clashes
🏛️

Political Instability

Government changes, civil unrest, protests, and political crises that create uncertainty about economic policies and contract enforcement. Affects investor confidence and capital flows.

Recent Examples
• Argentina currency crisis
• Turkey constitutional changes
• Myanmar military coup
📉

Economic Sanctions

Trade restrictions, financial sanctions, and economic embargoes that limit market access and currency convertibility. Can cause rapid devaluation of affected currencies.

Recent Examples
• Russian SWIFT exclusion
• Iran banking restrictions
• North Korea trade bans
🌊

Trade Disruptions

Supply chain interruptions, shipping route blockages, and trade route insecurity affecting commodity prices and export-dependent currencies. Often impacts AUD, CAD, and NOK.

Recent Examples
• Red Sea shipping crisis
• Panama Canal drought
• Suez Canal blockage (2021)
🔌

Energy Security

Oil and gas supply disruptions, pipeline sabotage, and energy export restrictions. Creates volatility in commodity currencies and inflation concerns globally.

Recent Examples
• Nord Stream pipeline damage
• OPEC+ production cuts
• Russian gas supply cuts to EU
💻

Cyber Warfare

State-sponsored cyber attacks on financial infrastructure, data breaches, and digital espionage threatening payment systems and banking stability.

Recent Examples
• SWIFT system attacks
• Central bank data breaches
• Cryptocurrency exchange hacks

Risk Management Strategies

Protect your portfolio during geopolitical uncertainty

1

Diversification

Spread risk across multiple currency pairs, asset classes, and geographic regions to reduce exposure to any single geopolitical event.

  • Maintain positions in safe-haven currencies (JPY, CHF, USD)
  • Balance commodity currencies with manufacturing economies
  • Include uncorrelated assets like gold and bonds
  • Limit exposure to any single region to 20% maximum
2

Position Sizing

Reduce position sizes during periods of elevated geopolitical risk to preserve capital and maintain trading flexibility.

  • Cut position sizes by 50% during high-risk periods
  • Use maximum 2% risk per trade rule strictly
  • Increase stop-loss distances to avoid noise
  • Maintain higher cash reserves for opportunities
3

Safe Haven Rotation

Shift capital to safe-haven currencies and assets when geopolitical tensions rise, protecting portfolio value.

  • Increase JPY and CHF allocations during crises
  • Consider USD strength in global risk-off scenarios
  • Add gold (XAU/USD) as portfolio insurance
  • Monitor VIX and gold/silver ratios for signals
4

Event-Driven Hedging

Use options and correlated pairs to hedge specific geopolitical exposures ahead of known risk events.

  • Buy protective puts on high-beta currencies
  • Use inverse correlations (e.g., oil up = CAD up)
  • Enter hedges 48 hours before major events
  • Close hedges immediately after event resolution
5

News Monitoring

Stay ahead of market-moving developments with real-time alerts and analysis from Goldenice Intelligence.

  • Set alerts for breaking geopolitical news
  • Monitor economic calendars for event risk
  • Follow official government statements
  • Use Goldenice's risk dashboard daily
6

Correlation Awareness

Understand how geopolitical events affect currency correlations and adjust strategies accordingly.

  • Track changing correlation matrices weekly
  • Recognize when safe havens move together
  • Avoid overexposure to correlated pairs
  • Exploit correlation breakdowns for alpha
