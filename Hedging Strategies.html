Strategy Details: Direct Hedge

A direct hedge involves opening a position in the opposite direction of your primary trade with the same currency pair. This is the most straightforward hedging technique, effectively neutralizing market exposure.

  • 100% protection against adverse moves
  • Eliminates profit potential in either direction
  • Incurs spread costs twice

When to Use

• High volatility events (NFP, CPI releases)
• Uncertain market direction
• Protecting existing profitable positions
• Weekend gap protection
