Corporate Governance

Building trust through transparency, accountability, and ethical leadership. Our governance framework ensures sustainable growth while protecting stakeholder interests.

✓ ISO 37001 Anti-Bribery Certified

Governance Pillars

⚖️

Accountability

Clear lines of responsibility from the Board to operational management. Regular performance evaluations and transparent reporting mechanisms ensure every decision is traceable and justifiable.

🔍

Transparency

Full disclosure of financial and non-financial information. Quarterly reports, annual general meetings, and real-time regulatory filings keep stakeholders informed.

🛡️

Risk Management

Enterprise-wide risk framework identifying, assessing, and mitigating operational, financial, and reputational risks. Independent Risk Committee oversight.

🤝

Fairness

Equal treatment of all shareholders and stakeholders. Protection of minority rights and zero-tolerance for discriminatory practices.

📋

Compliance

Adherence to international regulations including MiFID II, EMIR, and local financial authority requirements. Regular audits and compliance certifications.

🌱

Sustainability

ESG integration in decision-making. Environmental responsibility, social impact programs, and long-term economic viability are core to our strategy.

Governance Policies

📜 Code of Business Conduct and Ethics
▼

Our Code of Conduct establishes the ethical standards expected of all directors, officers, and employees. It covers:

  • Conflicts of interest disclosure and management
  • Insider trading prevention and market abuse regulations
  • Anti-bribery and corruption policies (zero tolerance)
  • Protection of confidential information and intellectual property
  • Fair dealing with clients, suppliers, and competitors
  • Reporting violations through whistleblower channels

Last Updated: January 2024 | Next Review: January 2025

📜 Board Diversity and Inclusion Policy
▼

Goldenice International is committed to maintaining a diverse Board that reflects the global markets we serve. Our targets include:

  • Minimum 40% gender diversity on the Board
  • At least one director from an ethnic minority background
  • Age diversity spanning multiple generations (35-70 years)
  • Geographic diversity representing key markets
  • Skills matrix covering finance, risk, technology, and regulatory expertise
  • Annual diversity reporting in Annual Report

Current Status: 50% female directors, 33% ethnic diversity

📜 Related Party Transactions Policy
▼

To prevent conflicts of interest, all related party transactions must undergo rigorous review:

  • Full disclosure of potential conflicts by directors annually
  • Independent director review of all related party transactions
  • Board approval required for transactions exceeding $50,000
  • Arm's length terms verification by external auditors
  • Quarterly reporting to Audit Committee
  • Public disclosure in financial statements per IFRS requirements

Compliance: 100% disclosure rate | Material Transactions: None in FY2024

📜 Executive Compensation and Clawback Policy
▼

Our remuneration philosophy aligns executive pay with long-term shareholder value and risk management:

  • Fixed pay capped at 40% of total compensation
  • Performance bonuses tied to 3-year rolling metrics
  • Deferred equity vesting over 5 years
  • Malus and clawback provisions for misconduct or restatements
  • Maximum pay ratio of 20:1 (CEO to median employee)
  • Annual shareholder advisory vote on remuneration

FY2024: 94% shareholder approval on remuneration report
