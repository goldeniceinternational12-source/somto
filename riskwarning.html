📉 Leverage and Margin Risks

▼

Goldenice International offers leveraged trading, which means you can control a large position with a relatively small amount of capital. While leverage can amplify profits, it equally amplifies losses.

Example of Leverage Risk: With 1:100 leverage, a 1% market movement against your position results in a 100% loss of your margin. A 2% movement could result in losses exceeding your initial deposit.

Margin Calls

You must maintain sufficient margin at all times. If your account equity falls below the required margin level, Goldenice International may close your positions without prior notice to prevent further losses.

Negative Balance

Due to extreme market volatility, losses may exceed your account balance. You may be liable for additional payments to cover deficit balances.

Stop-Out Level

Positions will be automatically liquidated when margin level reaches 20% or below to protect against further losses.

💱 Market and Liquidity Risks

▼

The foreign exchange market is affected by numerous factors including economic indicators, geopolitical events, central bank decisions, and market sentiment. These factors can cause rapid and substantial price movements.

  • Volatility Risk: Currency prices can change dramatically during news releases, economic announcements, or unexpected global events.
  • Gapping Risk: Markets may "gap" (jump from one price to another without trading at intermediate levels), causing stop-loss orders to execute at worse-than-expected prices.
  • Liquidity Risk: During certain market conditions (weekends, holidays, major news events), liquidity may dry up, resulting in wider spreads and slippage.
  • Weekend Risk: Positions held over weekends are subject to significant price gaps when markets reopen.
Important: Goldenice International is your sole counterparty for all trades. We act as market maker and may not always offer best execution prices available in the broader market.
