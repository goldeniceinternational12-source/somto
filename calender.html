Understanding Economic Indicators

Master the fundamentals of forex economic events

01

Employment Data

Non-Farm Payrolls (NFP) and unemployment rates are the most market-moving indicators. Released monthly, they signal economic health and influence central bank policy decisions.

02

Inflation Metrics

Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Producer Price Index (PPI) measure inflation. Higher inflation typically leads to currency appreciation as central banks raise interest rates.

03

Central Bank Decisions

Interest rate decisions, policy statements, and press conferences from Fed, ECB, BOE, and BOJ create significant volatility. Watch for forward guidance changes.

04

GDP Growth

Gross Domestic Product measures economic output. Quarterly releases show economic expansion or contraction, directly affecting currency strength and investor confidence.

05

Manufacturing Data

PMI (Purchasing Managers' Index) indicates manufacturing sector health. Values above 50 suggest expansion, below 50 indicates contraction. Leading indicator of economic trends.

06

Retail Sales

Consumer spending drives economic growth. Strong retail sales data boosts currency value as it signals economic confidence and potential inflationary pressures.
